Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 15 September 2026
Last Updated: 15 September 2026

Old Africa Adventures ("Old Africa Adventures", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, contacting us, requesting a quotation, or making enquiries about our travel services.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights available to you.

This policy applies to information collected through the Old Africa Adventures website and through enquiries initiated through our website, including email and WhatsApp.

1. Who We Are

Old Africa Adventures is a Kenya-based tourism and travel business offering services including safari packages, airport transfers, hotel bookings, mountain climbing, cultural tours, and customised travel experiences.

For questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, you can contact us at:

Email: oldafricaadventures@gmail.com

WhatsApp: +254 722 888 937

2. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following information:

Information you provide through our website

When you submit a contact or quotation request, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • WhatsApp or telephone number
  • Number of travellers
  • Trip type
  • Preferred travel dates
  • Message, special requests, or other information you voluntarily provide

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation, arrange travel services, or communicate with you about your request.

Information provided through WhatsApp

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, we may receive the information contained in your WhatsApp messages, including your name, telephone number, travel requirements, and any other information you choose to provide.

WhatsApp is a third-party communication service and its own privacy terms and policies may also apply.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be automatically collected by our hosting provider or other website technologies. Depending on the services enabled on the website, this may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location derived from technical information
  • Date and time of visits
  • Referring website or page

We may use this information to operate, secure, maintain, and improve our website.

We do not use this technical information to intentionally identify individual visitors unless necessary for security, legal, or operational purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the personal information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries and messages
  • Prepare and send travel quotations
  • Understand your travel requirements
  • Communicate with you about requested services
  • Arrange or coordinate travel-related services you request
  • Confirm or manage bookings
  • Respond to requests made through WhatsApp
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Protect our website and systems against fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

We will not use your information for purposes that are incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected unless permitted or required by applicable law.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal information on one or more of the following bases:

  • Your consent
  • Taking steps at your request before entering into a contract
  • Performing a contract or providing services you have requested
  • Compliance with a legal obligation
  • Our legitimate interests, where those interests do not override your privacy rights

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw that consent where applicable.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

5. Email and Enquiry Processing

When you submit our contact or quotation form, the information you provide is transmitted to our website backend and used to generate an enquiry email.

Our website backend uses Nodemailer to send enquiry emails to the Old Africa Adventures business email address.

Where you provide an email address, it may be included as the reply-to address so that we can respond directly to your enquiry.

We do not sell enquiry information to third parties.

6. WhatsApp Communication

Our website may provide an option to continue an enquiry through WhatsApp.

When you choose this option, your browser may open a WhatsApp conversation with Old Africa Adventures containing information from the form, such as your name, email address, telephone number, traveller count, trip type, travel dates, or other enquiry details.

You should review WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms before using the service.

If we enable automated WhatsApp notifications through a third-party provider such as Twilio, information submitted through our website may also be processed by that provider for the purpose of delivering the requested communication.

7. Service Providers and Third Parties

We may use trusted third-party technology providers to operate our website and communications.

Depending on the services enabled on our website, these may include:

  • Vercel — website hosting and deployment infrastructure
  • Google/Gmail or another SMTP email provider — delivery and receipt of enquiry emails
  • WhatsApp — customer communication when you choose to contact us through WhatsApp
  • Twilio — only where automated WhatsApp messaging is enabled
  • Other service providers reasonably necessary to provide requested travel or booking services

These providers may process personal information on our behalf or independently according to the nature of their services.

We do not permit service providers to use your personal information for purposes unrelated to the services they provide to us, except where otherwise permitted or required by law.

8. Travel and Booking Partners

If you request a travel service that requires us to work with another service provider, such as a hotel, transport provider, guide, activity provider, or other tourism partner, we may need to share relevant information with that provider to arrange the service.

We will seek to limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the requested service.

Where appropriate, we may also require our service providers to handle personal information securely and in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

9. International Data Transfers

Some technology providers we use may process or store information outside Kenya.

Where personal information is transferred outside Kenya, Old Africa Adventures will seek to ensure that the transfer is made in accordance with applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements, including appropriate safeguards, an applicable legal basis, or consent where required.

Kenya's data-protection framework contains specific requirements governing transfers of personal data outside Kenya.

Because Old Africa Adventures serves international travellers, information may also be exchanged with travel or accommodation providers in other countries when this is necessary to provide services you have requested.

10. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Responding to your enquiry
  • Preparing or managing a quotation
  • Providing requested travel services
  • Managing bookings and customer relationships
  • Meeting legal, accounting, tax, dispute-resolution, or regulatory requirements
  • Protecting our legitimate business interests

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, anonymise it, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include access controls, secure hosting, authentication, restricted access to business email accounts, and appropriate security practices for our website and systems.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant limitations, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right:

  • To be informed about how your personal information is used
  • To request access to personal information we hold about you
  • To request correction of inaccurate or misleading information
  • To object to certain processing of your information
  • To request deletion of personal information where applicable
  • To withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • To exercise other rights provided under applicable data-protection law

These rights reflect protections provided under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us using the details provided below.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children for independent marketing purposes.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorisation, please contact us so that we can assess and, where appropriate, delete the information.
