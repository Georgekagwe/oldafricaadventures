BORN FROM A LOVE OF THE WILD

Founded in 2015 by Samuel Kagwe, Old Africa Adventures began with a simple belief: that travel has the power to transform lives and strengthen our connection to nature and each other. Since then, we've guided thousands of travellers through Kenya's parks, coastlines and highlands — always in small groups, always led by people who grew up on this land.

Samuel grew up in a small village on the edge of Maasai Mara, where the rhythm of the seasons and the movement of the herds shaped daily life long before he ever thought of it as "wildlife." He spent his twenties driving and guiding for larger safari outfits across Kenya — learning the parks, the animals, and just as importantly, learning what most big tour companies get wrong: travellers becoming a name on a manifest, guides rotating in and out, and itineraries built around a spreadsheet rather than a person.

Old Africa Adventures was Samuel's answer to that. There's no call centre and no rotating roster of guides here — when you book a trip, you're speaking directly with the person who plans it, and more often than not, the person who drives you through the gate on day one. That's a deliberate choice: staying small enough that every guest gets his full attention, from the first WhatsApp message to the final airport drop-off.

It also means every itinerary is genuinely tailor-made. Because there's no head office dictating fixed packages, trips get adjusted around what you actually want to see, how you like to travel, and what the parks are doing that particular week — not a template written months in advance.

Over a decade later, that same philosophy still holds: fewer guests, deeper knowledge, and a level of care that only comes from someone who's personally invested in how your trip turns out — because his name is the one on it.
