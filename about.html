BORN FROM A LOVE OF THE WILD

Founded in 2016 by Samuel, Old Africa Adventures began with a simple belief: that travel has the power to transform lives and strengthen our connection to nature and each other. Since then, we've guided thousands of travellers through Kenya's parks, coastlines and highlands — always in small groups, always led by people who grew up on this land.
