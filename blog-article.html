Best Time to Visit Maasai Mara

Timing your safari can make all the difference. Here's your month-by-month guide to the Great Migration, wildlife viewing and weather in the Maasai Mara.

The Great Migration Calendar

July – October — Best for the Great Migration. Millions of wildebeest and zebra cross the Mara River in one of nature's most dramatic spectacles. Expect dry, sunny days and cooler nights.

January – March — Best for Calving Season. Predator action peaks as thousands of wildebeest calves are born on the plains, drawing lions, cheetahs and hyenas in close pursuit. Excellent light for photography.

April – June — The Green Season. Fewer crowds, lush landscapes and lower rates, though afternoon showers are common. A good choice for budget-conscious travellers who don't mind a little rain.

What to Pack

Neutral-coloured clothing, a warm layer for early morning game drives, sun protection, and a good pair of binoculars will serve you well whichever month you visit.
